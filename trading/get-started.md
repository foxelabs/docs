# Get Started

Welcome to the **Foxe Labs** documentation.

## What is Foxe Labs?

Foxe Labs builds **algorithmic trading systems and custom Expert Advisors (EAs)** for MetaTrader 5. Our focus is on automated strategies that put **risk management first** — tools that aim to keep losses small and consistent, with sensible defaults and built-in guardrails rather than dozens of dials to tune.

This site is the official home for our product documentation: installation guides, what each EA does, the settings you can adjust, and version history.

## Our Products

### Gold Scalpel

A precision **breakout scalper for MetaTrader 5**, pre-tuned for XAUUSD (Gold). It enters on confirmed breakouts, protects each trade by moving to breakeven early, then trails the stop to lock in gains — with percentage-risk sizing, an optional losing-streak cooldown, and an automatic weekend pause.
